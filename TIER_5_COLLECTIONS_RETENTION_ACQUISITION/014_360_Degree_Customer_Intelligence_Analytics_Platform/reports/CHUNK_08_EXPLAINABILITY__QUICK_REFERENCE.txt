KEY CONCEPTS:
================================================================================

FEATURE IMPORTANCE:
  = How much each feature contributes to predictions
  Example: EXT_SOURCE_3 (18.45%) >> DAYS_BIRTH (2.58%)
  Good if: Top 10 features explain 90%+ (interpretable)

CUMULATIVE IMPORTANCE:
  = How much top N features explain together
  Example: Top 3 = 35.89%, Top 10 = 95.23%
  Good if: Concentrated (top 10 explain 90%+)

FEATURE INTERACTIONS:
  = Combined effect of 2+ features
  Example: High credit × Low score = Very high risk
  Important: Explains non-linear relationships

CALIBRATION:
  = Do predicted probabilities match reality?
  Example: 60% predicted = ~60% actually default?
  Good if: Diff < 0.05 (well-calibrated)

MODEL BIAS:
  = Does model treat all groups fairly?
  Example: Same accuracy for males & females?
  Good if: No systematic bias, fair treatment
